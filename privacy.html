Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026
This is a general template, not legal advice. Before relying on it to protect your business, have it reviewed by a licensed attorney familiar with data-privacy law in the jurisdictions where your users live.

1. What we collect

  • Account info: name and email address, collected when you sign up.
  • Financial ledger data: the bills, income, debts, and business transactions you enter into your dashboard.
  • Billing info: handled directly by Stripe, our payment processor — we receive a customer/subscription ID but never your full card number.
  • Basic usage data: standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and reliability.

2. How we use it

We use your data solely to operate the Service: authenticating you, storing and displaying your ledger, calculating totals and projections, processing your subscription, and communicating with you about your account (e.g., trial ending, payment issues). We do not sell your data to third parties or use it for third-party advertising.

3. Where it's stored

Your account and ledger data are stored in a managed Postgres database (Supabase), protected by row-level security so only your authenticated account can read or write your own records. Payments are processed by Stripe, which maintains its own PCI-compliant infrastructure.

4. Who can see it

Only you can access your ledger data through your authenticated session. NextUp's operator may access account-level data (email, subscription status) to provide support or maintain the Service, but does not browse individual users' financial entries without a support-related reason.

5. Cookies & local storage

We use essential cookies/local storage to keep you signed in and remember your session. We don't use third-party advertising trackers.

6. Data retention & deletion

We retain your data while your account is active. If you cancel your subscription, your account and data remain accessible until you request deletion. To request full deletion of your account and data, email billpay@nextupcapitalgroup.com.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Contact us at the email below to exercise these rights.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service evolves. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email billpay@nextupcapitalgroup.com.
